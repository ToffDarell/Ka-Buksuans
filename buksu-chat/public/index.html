How Ka-Buksuan works

  1. Sign in with your BukSU email

    Use your student email (@student.buksu.edu.ph). Only Bukidnon State University students get in.

  2. Pick your college

    Choose your college, add your course if you want, and decide whether to match only with students from your college.

  3. Chat with a fellow Buksuan

    Start with video and text, or text only. Press Next to meet someone new, or Stop to leave.

  4. Stay safe

    Press Report if someone breaks the rules. Reported users can be banned.

Questions

Who can use Ka-Buksuan?

Only Bukidnon State University students with a BukSU student email (@student.buksu.edu.ph) can sign in.

Are video calls recorded?

No. Ka-Buksuan does not record or store video, audio or text chat. Read the Privacy Policy for the details.

Can I match only with students from my college?

Yes. Tick the option to match only with students from your college before you press Find Match.

Can I chat without video?

Yes. Choose Text Only in Match Setup to chat without using your camera or microphone.

What happens if someone breaks the rules?

Press Report during a chat. Users who harass, threaten or use hate speech or violent language can be banned.
