What Ka-Buksuan is

Ka-Buksuan is a random video and text chat for Bukidnon State University (BukSU) students. It is an unofficial student project. It is not run, endorsed or sponsored by Bukidnon State University, and nothing here speaks for the university.

Who can use it

Reports and bans

If someone breaks these rules, press Report during the chat and describe what happened. We review reports and may ban accounts that break the rules. A ban can be applied without warning, and we decide how serious a violation is. If you think a ban was a mistake, contact us through the Contact section and we will look at it.

Please do not share your home address, phone number, passwords, financial details or other sensitive information. You can press Stop or Next at any time. If you are in danger or someone commits a crime against you, contact the proper authorities.
