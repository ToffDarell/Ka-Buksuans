Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 20, 2026

Ka-Buksuan is a random video and text chat for students of Bukidnon State University (BukSU). This policy explains what personal information we handle when you use it, why, who else sees it, and what you can do about it. We wrote it in plain language on purpose.

Who we are

Ka-Buksuan is an unofficial student project. It is not run, endorsed or sponsored by Bukidnon State University. In this policy, “we” and “us” mean the student developer who runs Ka-Buksuan. You can reach us at the address in the Contact section.

What we collect

Your account

You sign in with Google. We receive your email address and an account ID from our sign-in provider. Google may also share basic profile details such as your name, and our provider may store them with your account.

Your match setup

Before a match you choose your college, optionally type your course, choose whether to match only with your own college, and choose Video + Text or Text Only. We use these to find you a match. They are held in the server’s memory while you wait or chat, and are not saved to a database. Your college and course are shown to the person you are matched with.

Video and audio

Video and audio go from your device to your match using WebRTC. We do not record them and we do not store them. Where a direct connection is not possible, the media is relayed through a third-party relay (TURN) server. Because of how WebRTC works, your match’s browser and these relay services can see your IP address during a video chat.

Text chat

Messages are passed through our server to your match in real time. We do not store them. Messages are limited to 500 characters and rate-limited to prevent spam. Keep in mind that the other person can copy, screenshot or record what you send.

Reports and bans

When you press Report, we save your account ID, the reported person’s account ID, the description you wrote (up to 500 characters) and an identifier for the chat session. We do not save chat messages or video, so we can only review what a report itself contains. If an account is banned, we keep a ban flag on that account’s record so the ban can be enforced.

Technical data

Our server keeps short-lived connection identifiers to run the chat. Our hosting provider may keep standard request logs such as IP address, browser type and time. Your browser stores your sign-in session and your light or dark theme choice on your own device.

What we do not collect

We do not use advertising trackers or analytics cookies, and we do not sell your personal information.

How we use it

  • To sign you in and keep your account secure.
  • To match you with another student the way you asked.
  • To review reports, ban accounts that break our rules, and keep the community safe.
  • To secure and troubleshoot the service and prevent abuse.
  • To answer your questions and requests.

Who else handles your data

  • Supabase provides sign-in and stores account, ban and report records.
  • Google provides the “Sign in with Google” step and STUN servers that help devices connect.
  • Render hosts the Ka-Buksuan website and server.
  • A TURN relay provider relays video and audio when a direct connection fails.
  • Your match sees your video, audio, messages, college and course, and can see your IP address during a video chat.
  • Authorities: we may share information with law enforcement or other appropriate authorities when the law requires it, or when it is needed to protect someone’s safety.

These providers process data under their own terms and policies. Some of them may store data outside the Philippines.

How long we keep it

Account records stay for as long as your account exists. Reports and ban records are kept for as long as they are needed to keep the community safe, then deleted or anonymised. Match setup details, video, audio and chat messages are not stored, so there is nothing to keep.

Your rights

Under the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173), you have the right to be informed, to access your personal data, to correct it, to object to its processing, to ask for it to be erased or blocked, to data portability, and to be compensated for damages from unlawful processing. To use any of these rights, contact us. If you think we have not handled your data properly, you can also complain to the National Privacy Commission at privacy.gov.ph.

Security

We use HTTPS, require sign-in for every chat connection and check each session on the server. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot promise absolute security. Please do not share sensitive personal details with people you meet on Ka-Buksuan.

Age

Ka-Buksuan is made for university students. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has used the service, contact us and we will remove the account.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page. If a change is important, we will also tell you on Ka-Buksuan.

Contact
